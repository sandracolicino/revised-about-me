About me

Hi there! Welcome to my first ever GitHub page. I am a Journalism student at CUNY - Craig Newmark School of Journalism. I started my interest in Journalism during my B.A. in Media Studies at Hunter College. While studying there, I took a considerable portion of my coursework in Journalism. A highlight from that time was the Neighborhood News class. My class was able to report from Hunts Point, a neighborhood in the South Bronx. This experience resulted in four published clips. I am now looking forward to having more published works during and after my time at the graduate school.

Best Journalism Pieces Of All Times

  1. The Watergate Scandal: It's a beautiful series of strong investigative reporting which culminated with the take down of a corrupt political system. If only history could repeat itself.
  2. The Pentagon Papers: This reporting unveiled the failures of the American government during the Vietnam war. Generations of politicians could have learned from this mistake but instead, the military industrial complex is one of the most profitable.
  3. The Wikileaks: Maybe it's a personal taste, but there's something special about classified information when they should be public knowledge. Some reporters go at great lenghts to tell the rest of us what people in positions of power don't wants to know.
